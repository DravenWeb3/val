The sequence

One route, no backtracking across the region.

Sikkim first (entry from the West Bengal border), then a loop through Assam picking up its Meghalaya spur, out to Arunachal's two halves, down through Nagaland–Manipur–Mizoram, closing in Tripura — positioned to hand off directly into Zone 2 (East India) next.

DRAVEN · FIELD NOTES

Zone 1 of 7 in the full India atlas. This covers Sikkim, Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram, Manipur, Nagaland, and Arunachal Pradesh at district level — major named destinations, not every village. Some Arunachal and Nagaland border-area sites need Inner Line Permits or Protected Area Permits arranged in advance; Mizoram requires an ILP for domestic visitors too.
