A.3 Digital-Asset & Web3 Risk

Crypto and leveraged/Web3 trading can result in partial or total loss. Crypto&Web3 options carry a high risk of rapid, total loss and are restricted in some jurisdictions — confirming legality where you reside is your own responsibility. Never risk money you cannot afford to lose.

  • Smart contracts — bugs, exploits, and oracle failures may cause irreversible loss.

Community Guidelines

Not a contract — a set of standards I hold the TTC community to, in my own words.

C.1 A note from me, directly

Everything above this section is the legal layer — necessary, but not how I actually want to talk to the people in this community. This part is that.

I built DravenWeb3 as a distribution channel for real signal, not noise. If you're here, I'm assuming you want the same thing: structure over hype, verification over vibes. The rules below aren't there to control you. They're there so the space stays useful for the next person who shows up looking for the same thing you were.

C.2 The rules

  • No guaranteed-return talk

    Not from me, not from you, not from anyone posting in these spaces. If a claim can't survive being checked, it doesn't belong here — that applies to community members exactly as much as it applies to me.

  • No services/product selling outside official channels

    If someone is DMing "guaranteed" calls, private groups, or asking for funds to manage on your behalf inside a DravenWeb3 space, that is not me and not sanctioned by me. Report it, don't engage with it.

  • Losses are yours, and so are the decisions

    I'll explain reasoning as clearly as I can. I won't pretend a loss was your fault for "not following instructions closely enough," and I won't take credit for a win that was your own risk call. Own the decision either way.

  • No harassment, doxxing, or personal-identity requests

    Don't ask other members — or me — for real-name, location, or personal identifying information. The pseudonym policy in A.11 applies to everyone here, not just me.

  • Disagreement is welcome; bad faith isn't

    Push back on my calls, my reasoning, my framework — that's the whole point of testing a claim against structure instead of authority. Bad-faith pile-ons, spam, and manipulation get removed regardless of who's right.

C.3 Enforcement

Violations may result in a warning, removal from a Community Space, or loss of paid access without refund, at my discretion. This section is a code of conduct, not a substitute for the Terms in Part A — where the two overlap, Part A governs.

Verify everything. Trust nothing that can't survive it.
