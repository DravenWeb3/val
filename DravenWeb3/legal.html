Everything above this section is the legal layer — necessary, but not how I actually want to talk to the people in this community. This part is that.

C.2 The rules

  • No guaranteed-return talk — about a token, protocol, or airdrop, from anyone, including me. If a claim can't survive being checked, it doesn't belong here.
  • No DM solicitation — if someone claiming to be me offers a "private" deal, asks to manage funds, or requests a payment outside official channels, that is not me. Report it, don't engage.
  • Losses and decisions are yours — I'll explain reasoning as clearly as I can. I won't blame a loss on you "not following closely enough," and I won't take credit for a win that was your own risk call.
  • No harassment, doxxing, or identity requests — don't ask other members, or me, for real-name or location information. The pseudonym policy in A.11 applies to everyone here.
  • Disagreement is welcome; bad faith isn't — push back on my reasoning, that's the point of testing a claim against structure instead of authority. Bad-faith pile-ons and manipulation get removed regardless of who's right.

C.3 Enforcement

Violations may result in a warning, removal from a Community Space, or loss of paid access without refund, at my discretion. This is a code of conduct, not a substitute for Part A — where the two overlap, Part A governs.

Verify everything. Trust nothing that can't survive it.
