DRAVEN
DECENTRALIZED EPISTEMOLOGIST

Draven is a sovereign builder and decentralized epistemologist working out of Dehradun, India — no institutional affiliation, no name or face attached to the work on purpose. Publishing happens under the pseudonym itself and under the open-source label Unspoken Indians for anything meant to belong to no one in particular.

The throughline across all of it is Contact Epistemology — the working rule that a claim earns belief by surviving contact with structure or physical consequence, not by citing who said it. That shows up as AI evaluation tooling (Draven Intel), as trading infrastructure (PulseFX, under TTC), as long-form structural writing (The Changeover, The Re-Entry, Sovereign Cognition), and as a route through India meant to be walked, not just mapped (India 528).

Two working modes, same person: builder mode — platforms, tools, smart contracts, content infrastructure — and thinker mode — the frameworks that scaffold what gets built. Everything on the main page is one or the other.
